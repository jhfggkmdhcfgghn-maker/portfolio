1. Sales & Commercial Performance Dashboard
Power BI DAX Modeling Star Schema

Business Problem

  • Raw sales transactions were siloed across different regional logs without unified reporting.
  • Executive leadership lacked real-time visibility into Year-over-Year (YoY) profit growth and top regional channels.

Technical Solution

  • Engineered a Star-Schema data model in Power BI, connecting fact tables with regional and product dimension tables.

Business Impact & Results

  • Decreased monthly reporting preparation time by 75% via automated data refresh.
  • Identified top 15% revenue-generating product categories, guiding strategic inventory reallocation.
