1. Sales & Commercial Performance Dashboard
Power BI DAX Modeling Power Query Star Schema

Business Problem

  • Raw transactional records were scattered across inconsistent regional logs.
  • Management lacked visibility into real-time Year-over-Year (YoY) profit growth and regional channel efficiency.

Technical Solution & DAX

  • Cleaned, transformed, and unpivoted raw data tables using Power Query.
  • Established a Star-Schema data model connecting Fact Sales with Dimension tables.
  • Wrote custom DAX measures for Time Intelligence, YoY Growth %, and Profit Margins.

Challenges & Solutions

  • Challenge: Slow dashboard render speeds due to circular data relationships.
  • Solution: Redesigned model schema to a single Star Schema and optimized DAX calculated measures.

Impact & KPIs

  • Automated dynamic visual dashboards replacing static monthly manual reporting.
  • Identified top-performing product categories and high-margin sales regions to focus marketing budget.
